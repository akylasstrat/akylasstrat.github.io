**Co-Lead with Dr Rui Tang · 30 credits · 2026–2027**

We jointly design, develop and teach the module material for a cohort of 15 students. The module covers machine learning, energy forecasting, thermal modelling, and optimization and control of buildings, PV and storage.

## BENV0092 — Energy Data Analytics in the Built Environment

**Lead · 15 credits · 2025–2027**

I lead material development, teaching and assessment design for more than 60 students on the Energy Systems and Data Analytics MSc. The module covers building energy data analysis, machine learning, load forecasting, thermal modelling and model predictive control.
